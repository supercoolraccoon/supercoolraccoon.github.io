Hermes

A personal AI assistant that runs on its owner's own home server.

What this application is

Hermes is a privately used software agent. It is not a public product and has no users other than its owner. It runs on a single personal Linux server and helps its owner with everyday tasks: monitoring that server, keeping research notes, and maintaining encrypted backups of its own configuration.

Why it asks for a Google account

The owner authorises Hermes with his own Google account, so that the agent can:

  • upload its encrypted configuration backups to a folder in the owner's Google Drive (the archive is encrypted with a password that only the owner holds — Google never sees its contents);
  • read and write the owner's own Google Docs, Sheets and Drive files when he explicitly asks for it (for example, “turn these notes into a document”).

No other person's data is accessed. There is no sign-up, no service, no third-party access.
